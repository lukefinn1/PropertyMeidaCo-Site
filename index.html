FAQ

The fine print, in plain English.

What does “unlimited photos” actually mean?

It means every room, angle and feature worth showing gets shot and edited. We don’t stop at a number and we won’t send you a bill for extras. A two-bed unit and a five-bed family home get the same promise.

How does the 24-hour guarantee work?

Your photos, including drone shots, are delivered within 24 hours of the shoot. If they’re late, the photography is free. The guarantee covers photos only. Video, reels and floor plans are delivered separately.

Do I need to be at the shoot?

No. Just let us know how to get access and whether anything is off-limits. We’ll take it from there.

What areas do you cover?

All of Cairns is included. Port Douglas, the Tablelands and Mission Beach are a flat $75 travel fee, and there’s no per-kilometre maths.
