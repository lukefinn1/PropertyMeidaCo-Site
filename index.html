FAQ

The fine print, in plain English.

What does “unlimited photos” actually mean?

Every room, angle and feature worth showing gets shot and edited. We don’t stop at a number and we won’t bill you for extras. A two-bed unit and a five-bed home get the same promise.

Which price applies to my listing?

It’s based on the number of bedrooms. Studios and 1–2 bedroom units sit in the first tier, 3–4 bedroom homes in the second, and 5 or more in the third. Not sure? Send us the address and we’ll confirm the price before the shoot.

Why are drone and staging one flat price?

Because you should know the total before you book. Drone and virtual staging are priced once for the whole shoot, not per image, so the invoice never grows with the photo count.

How does the 24-hour guarantee work?

Your photos, including drone shots, are delivered within 24 hours of the shoot. If they’re late, the photography is free. The guarantee covers photos only. Floor plans are delivered separately.

Do I need to be at the shoot?

No. Just let us know how to get access and whether anything is off-limits. We’ll take it from there.

What areas do you cover?

All of Cairns is included, from the Southside to the Northern Beaches (Trinity Beach, Palm Cove, Clifton Beach and Smithfield). Port Douglas, the Tablelands and Mission Beach are a flat $75 travel fee, with no per-kilometre maths.

Do you edit the skies?

Yes. Grey skies are replaced with natural-looking clear skies on every exterior and drone shot. We never alter the property itself.

What if I’m not happy with a photo?

Tell us which shot and what’s not right, and we’ll re-edit it free. No forms, no argument.

Can I use the photos after the sale?

Yes. You can use them to market that property, including on portals, social media and your own website, for as long as you need. Property Media Co keeps the copyright and may show the work in our portfolio.
